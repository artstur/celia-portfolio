Problem Space

You’ve planned two walks. One is a reminder to catch up on exercise. The other is a date with a friend. By the time you’ve met your weekly goal, only one of those walks has lost its purpose.

I designed Along to keep track of why a task exists. It can suggest a change, explain what prompted it, and leave the decision with you.

01. Which Walk Would You Cancel?

In this simulated week, four exercise sessions add up to 195 minutes against a goal of 180. Along proposes removing the catch-up reminder. The person can accept the edit or keep the original tasks.

Plan review

02. Inside the Prototype

From a goal to the next ten minutes

You can describe a new goal in your own words, including your timing and preferences. The exercise demo starts with a goal of 120 minutes of movement this week.

With 75 minutes logged, the plan has 45 minutes left. Add a 35-minute workout and Along shortens the suggested walk to ten minutes.

What the agent gets to see

On iPhone, the person chooses which Apple Health summaries to share. Along sends the selected observations and the current plan to a paired Mac for review. The web demo uses sample data.

In one iPhone check, the observations matched the last review. Along kept all three existing tasks.

03. Agent Evaluation

What did Along know at the time?

I shaped an evaluation around 54 synthetic decision cases across exercise, savings and Cantonese practice. Each replay gives the agent only the information available when it has to decide what to do next.

In the Cantonese listening test, a learner understands four of five new phrases and answers one of three aloud. Change one detail and the next practice session should change with it.
